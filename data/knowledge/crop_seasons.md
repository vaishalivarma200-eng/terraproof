# Indian Agricultural Crop Seasons & Phenology Calendar

## 1. The Three Primary Agricultural Seasons

### Kharif Season (Monsoon / Autumn Crops)
- **Sowing Period:** June to July (synchronized with the onset of the southwest monsoon).
- **Harvesting Period:** September to November.
- **Key Crops:** Paddy (Rice), Soybean, Maize, Cotton, Bajra (Pearl Millet), Jowar, Groundnut, Arhar (Pigeon Pea).
- **Vulnerability:** Highly susceptible to excess rainfall, embankment breaches, localized flash flooding, and waterlogging during July and August.

### Rabi Season (Winter / Spring Crops)
- **Sowing Period:** October to December (post-monsoon soil moisture retention).
- **Harvesting Period:** March to May.
- **Key Crops:** Wheat, Mustard / Rapeseed, Gram (Chickpea), Barley, Peas, Lentils.
- **Vulnerability:** Susceptible to unseasonal hailstorms, early terminal heat stress, and frost during January through March.

### Zaid Season (Summer Crops)
- **Sowing Period:** March to April.
- **Harvesting Period:** May to June.
- **Key Crops:** Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Vegetables, Fodder crops.

## 2. Phenological Anomaly & Discrepancy Detection
- **Seasonal Conflict Rule:** Wheat cannot be cultivated or damaged in standing floodwaters in July in India. Wheat is harvested by April/May; fields in July are either fallow, prepared for paddy, or planted with Kharif crops.
- A claim citing "Wheat" flood loss in July represents a categorical agronomic discrepancy, often arising from clerical errors, erroneous portal selection, or fraudulent filing.
