# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Operational Guidelines

## 1. Overview and Core Purpose
The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is the Government of India's flagship crop insurance scheme providing comprehensive financial support to farmers against non-preventable natural perils.

## 2. Localized Calamities Assessment
Under PMFBY revised guidelines, localized calamities include:
- Hailstorm
- Landslide
- Inundation / Flash Flooding
- Cloudburst
- Natural Fire

Individual farm-level assessment is permitted for localized perils like flood and hailstorm when intimated within 72 hours of the occurrence.

## 3. The 33% Loss Threshold Rule
- A minimum crop loss of 33% at the individual field or insured unit level is the statutory threshold required to trigger insurance compensation under localized calamities.
- When satellite-observed damage is subtle (e.g., 10% to 15%), remote sensing cannot definitively confirm whether loss exceeds the 33% threshold. In such cases, PMFBY guidelines mandate joint physical ground inspection.

## 4. Indemnity & Payout Calculation Formula
Compensation payable to an insured farmer is calculated as:
`Payout (₹) = Sum Insured per Hectare (₹) × Observed Damaged Area (ha) × Severity Rate (%)`
Where:
- Sum Insured per Hectare is fixed per crop per district by the State Level Coordination Committee on Crop Insurance (SLCCCI) (typically ₹30,000 to ₹45,000/ha for paddy).
- Observed Damaged Area cannot exceed the farmer's legally registered or cultivated parcel area.
- Severity Rate represents the percentage of yield loss (e.g., 80% for severe lodging/siltation).

## 5. Non-Preventable Natural Risks vs. Human Fraud
Insurance covers only involuntary natural losses. Claims involving intentional burning, artificial inundation, unauthorized land-use change, or inflated area reporting are held for investigation by the District Monitoring Committee.
